Pasta Recipe

Description

This is a simple and delicious pasta recipe that you can make at home.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil leaves

Instructions

  1. Boil water and cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the tomato sauce in a pan and add the minced garlic.
  3. Once the pasta is cooked, drain it and add it to the sauce.
  4. Mix well and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Garnish with fresh basil leaves before serving.
